Planned safeguards

Built around trust & safety

When we launch, every professional on the platform will be independently verified and licensed.

PCN & MDCN verified professionals

All pharmacists and doctors will hold valid licences from Nigeria's regulatory bodies before going live.

Private & documented

Consultations will be confidential. Records kept for continuity of care, not shared without consent.

Response within 15–30 minutes

Target response times during business hours (9am–7pm WAT), including follow-ups within 7 days.

Important disclosure

CarePath is a prototype, not a live service

  • No medical professionals are currently available. This website is a concept demonstration. Anyone responding via WhatsApp is a member of the founding team, not a licensed clinician.
  • Regulatory approvals are pending. CarePath has not yet received the licences required to operate as a healthcare service in Nigeria. We are actively working through the registration process with MDCN and PCN.
  • Do not make health decisions based on this platform. If you have a medical concern, please visit a registered clinic, pharmacy, or hospital in Lagos.
  • We welcome your feedback. We're speaking with potential users to shape the product. Reach us at thelogicbaseng@gmail.com — your input matters.
